Our Vision & Objectives

Guiding principles that help strengthen the IT community of Banswara.

👁️ VISION

  • Strengthen the IT community of Banswara.
  • Promote collaboration, innovation and knowledge sharing.
  • Encourage professional growth and skill development.
  • Create a supportive and inclusive environment for all members.
  • Build a strong network of IT professionals across the region.

🎯 OBJECTIVE

  • Represent and support the interests of IT professionals.
  • Organize technical events, workshops and seminars.
  • Facilitate learning, mentoring and career opportunities.
  • Promote transparent communication among members.
  • Encourage leadership, teamwork and community engagement.

How to Join the Union

1

Verify Eligibility

Government IT employees working in Banswara district can apply.

2

Submit Membership Request

Contact the union office or any executive committee member.

3

Provide Required Details

Share your designation, department and contact information.

4

Verification

The committee will review your application.

5

Pay Membership Fee

Deposit the prescribed membership fee as communicated by the union office.

6

Membership Confirmation

Your membership will be activated after successful verification and fee submission.
